1Myrmex — sprite set, observer screen and panels (brief v1.7)
Assumptions
No engine assets exist yet, so every sprite here is drawn as a tint-ready vector at 128 px per cell and rendered through one shared world model — day, night, the zoom ladder and the minimap all come from the same data, which is how the engine will do it. Type is IBM Plex Sans / Plex Mono (the brief names none). Brief amended to v1.7: bestiary redesigned in a Zerg-like direction (original creatures, no Blizzard silhouettes) — four-legged raiders with bone scythes and exposed muscle, bloated brood mother with egg sacs, tentacled lurker, tusked brute, winged shade; the nest is living creep with veins, glowing nodules and bone spines. v1.6: final art is hand-painted raster over these vector composition sheets (team-colour mask for roles), the queen chamber is a dome with the queen on its threshold, trees are sparse passable decor, frame grain at 13%. v1.5 (StarCraft-1 camera): every sprite — creatures included — is drawn from one high front camera (0.72 Y-foreshortening, bodies lifted over flat feet shadows, 56/128 vertical faces, flat selection ellipse); ground uses four noise-placed tints with striated texture, water has shore and deep variants. Rendering follows the WarCraft/StarCraft school: dark contour lines, sphere and top/front gradient shading, textured ground with trodden earth around the nest, bevelled HUD frames with a clay trim. Two palette corrections written back as finals: worker tint moves from #85A363 (invisible on ground) to bone #D9D2B4, carrier to #E09A5C for the same reason; ground settles at #8FAE6C. Scene is a 256² world with the nest ring at (114–141, 114–137): entry hall from the north gap, two food halls west, two resource halls east, a walled throne hall with the brood mother at (127,131), a nursery to the south, side galleries to the east gap.
Design reasoning
¾ volume comes from a −25% front face + soft shadow on walls, rocks, storages; the grid stays square. Storages read their fill state at 16 px through size (food: growing disc; resources: horizontal gauge) rather than fine detail. Role tints differ in lightness (worker light bone, builder mid gold, guard dark blue) so the night overlay cannot merge them. HUD chrome is dark bevelled panels (#2E2C27→#1A1916 gradient, clay trim) kept to the edges so the bright world stays the hero; nothing in the HUD touches structures.
